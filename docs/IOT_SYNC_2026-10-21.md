# IoT and product meetings, 20 and 21 Oct 2026

What the two meeting transcripts say that bears on the app. Only what was said is listed. Nothing here changes the confirmed BLE contract.

## Product direction (John)

- V1 does not need real-time streaming. A snapshot or a recorded clip plus a question is enough, phased in later. Examples given: "what is this building", and a photo of food with "tell me the ingredients".
- The bar: whatever a person does today with ChatGPT on the phone (take a picture, ask about it) CAIPO must at least do.
- V1 has no screen. The phone app is the screen: the user should be able to see what CAIPO is looking at.
- Use existing models through FloBrain. No model training.
- The data path is open: CAIPO to the app to FloBrain, or the Raspberry Pi straight to FloBrain. The team should measure the latency of each before choosing.
- Starting with the phone camera instead of CAIPO's was raised in the discussion.

What the app did with this: Ask about a photo (Conversation screen). Photo from the phone, or the last picture CAIPO sent over R03, plus a typed question. See `docs/STATUS.md`, Photo questions. Live FloBrain does not pass the image to the model yet, so live builds block it.

## Hardware state (Antoine, Sabri)

- Tested one component at a time, powered from a PC over USB: LED, touch sensor, microphone, speaker.
- Microphone works. Speaker output quality is bad. Two microphones for noise cancellation were suggested for study.
- The touch sensor reports single tap, double tap, and long press.
- The LEDs are GRBW, not RGB, despite the product listing.
- Proposal: two LEDs. One for state (recording, transfer, AI working). One for power (charging, low battery). The PMIC has a pin that can drive a plain LED for charging.
- Power management circuit is redesigned (Texas Instruments parts) but nothing is built. Battery level will come from a fuel gauge.
- Next step is the state machine that ties the parts together. Power use and heat still need measuring.
- The camera has not been tested. The team does not have it yet.
- The ESP32 stays on at low power. It wakes the Raspberry Pi for heavy work (camera, AI), and the Pi sleeps again after.

## Firmware state (Sosina, Mohamed, Ash)

- Sosina wrote the ESP32 BLE layer and pushed it to her branch. It has not run on a real ESP32. Wokwi has no Bluetooth.
- ESP32 to Raspberry Pi over UART was tried in Wokwi.
- A handover document for the BLE layer exists. Mohamed will test BLE with the app once he has the protocol details and a board.

## What the app does not change because of this

- LED colors in `lib/shared/theme/caipo_tokens.dart` stay as they are. GRBW and the two-LED split are hardware decisions; no LED command opcode is confirmed.
- Gestures stay `Needs device support`. Single, double and long press exist on the sensor, but no gesture event is in the BLE contract.
- Battery stays the standard Battery Service. The fuel gauge feeds it on the device side.
- No live view picture. The stream stays behind `PROPOSED_STREAM`.
